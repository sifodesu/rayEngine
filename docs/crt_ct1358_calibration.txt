Hitachi CT-1358 calibration contract
====================================

The emulator separates documented NTSC constants from parameters that must be
measured on one physical television. crt_params.json must remain marked
provisional_unmeasured until all measurements below refer to the same serial
number and control settings.

Required measurements
---------------------

1. Record composite input and LA7621 video/chroma outputs with PLUGE, SMPTE
   bars, multiburst and a zone plate. Fit luma/chroma bandwidth, group delay,
   peaking, clamp, AGC attack/release, horizontal AFC, ACC, differential
   gain/phase, burst PLL bandwidth and color-killer thresholds.
2. Measure R/G/B luminance at 16 drive levels with a colorimeter. Fit gain,
   cutoff and gamma, then derive the three tubeColorMatrix rows from XYZ
   measurements of the primaries and white.
3. Photograph the A34JLN60X faceplate orthogonally with a calibrated macro
   scale. Measure triad pitch, slot aspect, row stagger and black-matrix duty.
4. Record isolated R/G/B impulses with a fast photodiode. Fit the fast/slow
   decay constants and weights of all three temporal components without the
   observer integration stage.
5. Measure geometry, focus and convergence grids at 10%, 50% and 100% APL.
   Record raster expansion versus APL and fit highVoltageBloom plus
   highVoltageResponse. Measure nominal anode voltage with a rated high-voltage
   probe under the documented safety procedure; never infer it from the service
   ceiling.
6. Measure the optical point-spread function from dim and bright isolated spots
   in a dark room and per primary, then fit RGB bloom radii, wide bloom,
   halation, faceplate dispersion and saturation independently.
7. Calibrate the host display with a colorimeter. Record peak luminance, black
   level, EOTF and primary chromaticities; an uncalibrated host display cannot
   reproduce the measured tube radiance absolutely. The provisional SDR
   presentation uses 160 nits rather than a panel-advertised maximum: an
   ordinary PNG/backbuffer carries no HDR luminance metadata, so treating a
   nominal 250-nit panel maximum as SDR reference white compresses the tube
   into a visibly dark and dull code range.
8. With the simulator's half-white reference pattern settled, measure the mean
   linear pre-output radiance of the white half and store it as
   referenceWhiteRadiance. This converts the arbitrary internal beam/phosphor
   units into the front-of-faceplate tubePeakNits measurement. It must not be
   replaced by an extra brightness boost, which would count mask and glass
   losses twice and make the result depend on the host display peak.

Implemented physical state model
--------------------------------

- The source is native progressive 240p: 262 complete lines per frame, 240
  active lines and a 60.054443 Hz frame clock
  derived directly from the 15.734264 kHz line clock. Carrier phase repeats at
  every frame boundary because the progressive line count is even.
- The 320x192 framebuffer occupies 192 source-bearing lines (44 through 235);
  the other active electrical lines remain setup black
  and presented in its native 5:3 aspect. There is no 4:3 letterbox and no
  overscan crop; the complete source rectangle remains visible.
- Raster strikes use the physical active line and horizontal active-video
  time. The endpoint state and displayed exposure are two outputs of the same
  closed-form sum of beam impulses. Display exposure is integrated over one
  complete 60.054 Hz CRT cycle, not the jittering host-frame duration, so the
  raster cannot alias into whole-image flicker or a rolling dark band. The
  geometric sum remains exact after frame skips without a fixed loop limit.
- The engine framebuffer is double-buffered at progressive vertical blank.
  Prior/current emission histories preserve the correct image when one host
  update interval crosses one or several frame boundaries, including
  top-to-bottom beam race and low host frame rates.
- Composite sync is not assumed after encoding: separate persistent horizontal
  AFC and vertical-oscillator states measure the actual waveform. The vertical
  loop detects the three serrated broad-sync lines and its phase determines
  which source raster line the decoder displays.
- The physical CT-1358 path remains identified as a no-comb receiver. For the
  320x192 presentation profile, ntscLineCombStrength optionally blends a
  two-line software comb after the chroma band-pass. Its default 0.90 suppresses
  most monochrome cross-colour in multiburst and zone-plate tests while leaving
  only a small residual no-comb signature; set it to zero for strict hardware
  fidelity.
- Cathode cutoff, RGB output bandwidth, gun power law, space charge and beam
  current limiting are solved before electron deflection.
- EHT and B+ use separate load-dependent attack/recovery states. Prior/current
  drive histories make their load follow the progressive spot instead of
  changing the whole screen at once. Their voltage drives raster size, focus,
  cathode headroom and phosphor efficiency.
- Every persistent analog state receives actual wall-clock elapsed time. A
  suspend, debugger stop or dragged window therefore settles phosphor, AGC,
  PLL, EHT, B+ and thermal memory instead of preserving 250 ms slices of stale
  history. Mains ripple is sampled at each pixel's raster time, not at a
  spatial surrogate or one global host-frame phase.
- Mains hum and rail ripple remain implemented at raster time but default to
  zero while the profile is unmeasured. Enabling guessed asynchronous ripple
  would manufacture a moving hum bar rather than reproduce this CT-1358.
- Slot-mask heating is a persistent low-resolution thermal field with lateral
  diffusion and local doming/landing error.
- The faceplate includes a curved surface normal, Snell displacement,
  angle-dependent Fresnel loss, Beer-Lambert absorption and internal return.
- Absolute luminance uses the spatial mean of the settled 50%-APL white
  reference. tubePeakNits therefore describes light measured in front of the
  faceplate, while referenceWhiteRadiance removes only the arbitrary scale of
  the internal current/radiance units. Mask texture, scan structure, APL sag,
  glass angle and vignetting keep their relative physical effects.

These equations are physically structured but their tube/circuit coefficients
remain provisional until the measurements above are performed on one CT-1358.
The service supplement does not establish a measured nominal anode voltage for
this unit, so the profile deliberately stores null rather than the former 22 kV
estimate. Likewise, rayEngine-320x192-262p names this engine's fixed source
timing; it is not a claim that every console's informal "240p" timing is equal.

Reproducibility
---------------

- Warm the set for at least 30 minutes.
- Record serial number, brightness, contrast, color, tint, room illuminance,
  probe model, camera exposure and input source levels.
- Keep raw captures outside the generated profile and version their checksums.
- Run `python3 scripts/validate_crt_profile.py --require-measured` before
  claiming a measured profile.
